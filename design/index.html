This replaces the saved demonstration state, including responses, verifications, recommendations, decisions, and meeting requests, with the original record.

CivicPath is the city’s system of record for every development approval, from pre-application to certificate of occupancy. It shows only what the city can know: city actions as authoritative record, filings as filed, outside decisions once uploaded and verified, and nothing about private business facts. This demonstration follows Saguaro Commons, a project with prior approvals on its parcel, through seven personas.

  1. Resident. Factual status, approvals on the parcel, public documents only.
  2. Reviewers. Draft comments, Staff only, on sheets C-03 and SP-01.
  3. Case manager. Assemble and issue the one comprehensive correction request; record non-binding staff assessments; verify an uploaded outside decision.
  4. Applicant. Request a correction meeting (city must meet within 10 working days of the request), keep a private team checklist, submit one coordinated response.
  5. City manager. Time frames, correction requests per application, refund exposure, inactive applications with observable reasons only.

City of Cholla Ridge, Mesquite County, Cholla Ridge Water Company, all companies, people, addresses, parcels, ordinances, and project data are fictional. State agencies appear by name for illustration only and do not use or endorse CivicPath. Fees and time frames are samples. Persona switching illustrates the experience, not authentication. The assistant is simulated.
